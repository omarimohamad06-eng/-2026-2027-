## Structural decisions
- Slide 6’s “Problèmes de l’unité” fill the dotted lines of the cahier’s page 1 (the reference leaves them blank).
- Slide 7 (*Introduction* + 2 questions) gives the “Problèmes du chapitre”. In the reference they come from a different text with 3 questions.
- Slide 96 (Doc 3, fermentation lactique) is placed as **partie 3)** after the rendement part. In the slides it comes before part 2. Moving it keeps the document numbering of Activité 6 in order (Doc 1 → Doc 2 → Doc 3).
- Slide 58 (complement to Doc 3 p. 6) becomes **Document 4** of Activité 3 in a cahier-style frame.
- The portrait of H. A. Krebs (slide 62) and the blank version of the 38 ATP diagram (slide 89) are left out, as in the reference.
- Page references of the cahier (“doc 1 p 2”, “doc 3 p 9” …) are dropped because the documents are now inside the lesson. The reference does the same.
